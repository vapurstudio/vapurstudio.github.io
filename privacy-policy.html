Mahjong Joy 3D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Mahjong Joy 3D ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. We respect your privacy.

Advertising

To support the developer, the app shows ads only at natural breaks: an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a level-complete screen (not during your first levels, and at most every few levels), and optional reward videos that you choose to watch for bonus coins. There are no banner ads. Buying "Remove ads" turns off the full-screen ads for good. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first (via Google's consent form) and can change their choice at any time with the Privacy button on the level map. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Data the developer collects

None. The developer does not run any servers and does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts and no analytics. The app's own features work offline.

In-app purchase

The app offers one optional purchase, "Remove ads", processed by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details; Google Play handles payment under the Google Payments privacy notice. The app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your level progress, stars, coins, unlocked themes and mascots, your island (crystals, house, decorations and blocks), your sound, vibration and language settings, whether you bought "Remove ads", and when an ad was last shown, locally on your device. This data never leaves your device (other than through Android's own device backup, if you have it enabled) and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer at the email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
